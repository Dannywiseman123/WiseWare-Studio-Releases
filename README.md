# WiseWare Studio releases

Signed update files for WiseWare Studio. The app checks here by itself, downloads new versions and only installs them after checking WiseWare's signature.

## Newest version: 2.7.0

- Every 2026 pack now has four colour themes. Pick one in Customise, Look. The alerts, chat, emote shower and recent events follow your choice.
- A sixth scene in every new pack: Offline, for when you are not live. Garage closed, game saved, tape ejected and more.
- Your socials (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Kick, Discord, Bluesky, Twitch) on Starting Soon, BRB, Ending and Offline. Add them in Customise, Details.
- Big moments fill the screen: raids, 5 or more gifted subs, 1,000 bits or more and tips of 20 or more. Turn it off on the Alerts page.
- A better Store: browse by category (Cosy, Cute, Sport, Retro, Sci-fi, Horror, Tabletop, Loud and Bright, Minimal), filter by what you stream and by light or dark, search, and sort. Every card shows its colour themes.
- Chat box, goal bar, emote shower and recent events styles now come with the packs you own.
- Sticker Bomb is now called Scrapbook Pop.
- New on the Studio page: an Emote shower that rains your chat emotes on screen in your pack style, and a Recent events list.
- New alert sounds: every pack has its own tune, with its own gift and tip sounds too.
- A sharper look all round: a lapping car on Pit Wall, dice that re-roll on Game Night, a walking hero on Pocket Quest, a volume bar on Rewind, a satellite on Mission Control, something in the hallway on Night Shift, stickers that boing, popping sound effects on Comic Strip and a glass shine on Clean Slate.

**New to WiseWare Studio?** Download [WiseWare-Studio-Starter.exe](https://github.com/Dannywiseman123/WiseWare-Studio-Releases/raw/main/WiseWare-Studio-Starter.exe) (about 6 MB) and run it. It downloads the newest version, checks WiseWare's signature and installs it.

**Already have it?** It updates by itself. You can also press Check for updates in Settings.
